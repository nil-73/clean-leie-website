Privacy notice

Short version: you can report without giving us any personal data. If you leave your email, we only use it to contact you about your report.

Who we are

Clean Leie is a student project by HOGENT E-lab students. Contact: team email address

What we collect

  • Report details: the problem type, the spot (and map pin if you place one), date and time, your description and photo, and whether you're a business, local or tourist. This is not personal data unless you put personal details in the description or photo, so please don't.
  • Business name (optional).
  • Email (optional), only with your consent.

Photos are cleaned in your browser before upload: location data and other hidden information (EXIF) are removed.

Why

To see where and when the canals have problems, to show the results in our school project, and, only if you gave your email, to ask you a follow-up question about your report. Your email is never shown publicly, shared or used for newsletters.

Where it's stored and for how long

Reports are stored in a database hosted in the EU (Supabase, Frankfurt region). Only the project team can read them. We delete email addresses when the project ends, e.g. 30 June 2027. We may keep reports without personal data as anonymous statistics.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing team email address. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit).

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no tracking. The map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.
